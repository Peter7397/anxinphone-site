Privacy Policy

Anxin Phone (安心手机) · Last updated: August 16, 2026

Provided by 陈平根 (Pinggen Chen). Anxin Phone helps a caregiver configure an iPhone for elder simplification or student focus. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

Data we access on the device

  • Contacts: When call-protection sync is enabled, favorite names and numbers configured by the caregiver are written into a local Contacts group so known callers can be recognized.
  • Location: Used only when the user taps SOS, optionally to include an Apple Maps link in an emergency SMS draft.
  • Camera & Photos: Used for the elder album and optional SOS photo attachments. Photos stay on the device.
  • Notifications: Local alarms after you grant notification permission.
  • Screen Time: Family Controls authorization is used to apply and schedule app shields via Apple’s APIs.
  • No account required: Anxin Phone does not require you to create an account on our servers.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We do not read live SMS or WeChat messages. Message previews in Elder mode are text entered by the caregiver in settings.

How data is stored

Settings, schedules, PIN, alarms, and caregiver-entered contacts are stored locally on the device (including an App Group shared with Screen Time extensions). We do not operate a backend that receives this personal data.

Children & caregivers

The app is designed to be set up by a parent or caregiver on the device that will be used daily. We do not knowingly collect children’s data on our servers because we do not operate such servers for this app.

Third parties

The app may open system or installed apps (Phone, Messages, Shortcuts, Find My, WeChat). Those products have their own privacy policies. Anxin Phone is not affiliated with Apple, Tencent, or WeChat.

Your choices

You can revoke permissions in iOS Settings, clear caregiver-entered data in the app, or uninstall the app to remove local Anxin Phone data from the device.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.

隐私政策

安心手机（Anxin Phone） · 更新日期：2026年8月16日

由陈平根提供。安心手机帮助家人在同一台 iPhone 上完成老人简化模式或学生专注模式的设置。本政策说明应用如何使用和处理相关数据。

我们在设备上访问的数据

  • 通讯录：开启来电保护同步时，会将看护人配置的常用联系人写入本地通讯录分组，以便识别熟人来电。
  • 位置：仅在用户点击 SOS 时使用，可选将 Apple 地图链接写入紧急短信草稿。
  • 相机与照片：用于老人相册及可选的 SOS 照片附件，照片保存在本机。
  • 通知：在您授权后用于本地闹钟。
  • 屏幕使用时间：用于通过 Apple API 应用与安排应用屏蔽。
  • 无需注册账户：无需在我们的服务器上创建账户。

我们不会

  • 出售您的个人数据
  • 使用第三方广告或分析 SDK
  • 在其他公司的应用或网站中跟踪您
  • 读取真实短信或微信内容；老人模式中的信息预览由看护人在设置中填写

数据如何存储

设置、日程、PIN、闹钟与联系人信息仅保存在设备本地（含与扩展共享的 App Group）。我们不为该应用运营接收此类个人数据的后端。

儿童与看护人

本应用设计为由家长或看护人在日常使用的设备上完成设置。我们不会在服务器上故意收集儿童信息，因为本应用不为此目的运营服务器。
